# Marianne search

Use the sibling `research` skill bundle and its engagement-selection decision first.
Recurring situated research belongs with a suitable existing person and qualified
provided engagement; a new subject, date, model or timeout does not require a new
score or global aliases. Supply the assignment through the supported task/cadenza
surface and preserve life. Qualified ephemeral work remains appropriate when
continuity adds no value.

When a named adaptive-search behavior requires generation, follow research's
conditional generator and venue-owner installation route. Commands and resources
are relative to the research bundle, not this shim. This entry grants no daemon
maintenance authority and does not supply an alternative installation recipe.

The score preserves indexed context, searches complementary domains, selects zero to two material followups, and delivers a checked natural answer with inline primary citations. Partial outcomes remain explicit. This shim replaces public A/B selection; historical comparisons stay outside the package.
