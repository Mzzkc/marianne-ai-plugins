# Direct and Monitor

## Directive contract

Every consequential directive states:

- **effect** — what must become true and why it matters to the vision;
- **recipient** — accountable owner and delegated authority;
- **context** — source material, prior decisions, constraints, and where they
  can be found;
- **coordination** — peers, principals, consumers, and collision surfaces;
- **proof** — observable behavioral or artifact evidence of adoption;
- **timing** — urgency, dependencies, checkpoint, and escalation condition;
- **status** — active, superseded, accepted, rejected, or blocked.

Issue the directive directly or through principals, managers, co-conductors,
cadenzas, queues, or venue-native artifacts. Then verify that it reached every
layer whose behavior must change. “Acknowledged,” “understood,” and “will do”
are not proof.

Communication is two-way. Musicians are experts: invite challenges, missing
context, proposed alternatives, and peer coordination. Resolve disagreements
at the lowest competent level unless they alter the vision, libretto, shared
contract, risk, or long-term direction.

## Monitoring rhythm

Use the lightest combination that keeps the world model current:

- direct report reading;
- event or status monitoring;
- principals and alignment sentinels;
- governed pull queues;
- scheduled checkpoints;
- exception-based alerts.

Watch trajectory and evidence, not message volume. Track:

- idle or polling musicians;
- blocked work and the age of each blocker;
- context that failed to reach its recipient;
- duplicate effort and ownership gaps;
- dependency or resource collisions;
- drift from vision or venue;
- promised evidence not produced;
- corrections acknowledged but not adopted;
- deferred work disappearing from view;
- producers grading their own work without independence.

The conductor's attention is scarce. Restore useful work to idle experts,
delegate observation, and avoid becoming the slowest serial worker in a
parallel system.

## Status judgment

Report:

1. what end state moved;
2. what evidence proves it;
3. what interaction or side effect changed;
4. what remains unresolved;
5. what direction was issued next.

File counts, commits, green local jobs, and enthusiastic reports are activity
signals. They become progress only when joined to the vision and its evidence.
