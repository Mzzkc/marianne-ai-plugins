# Marianne Operations from the Podium

## Use the supported engagement interface

Ordinary conducting uses the venue's offered musicians, stock engagements and
job controls. State the intended outcome, scope, relevant context, priorities
and acceptance criteria through the supported interface, including cadenza where
appropriate. Prefer the provided full lifecycle, targeted work and lifecycle
integration shapes. A novel subject does not itself require a custom score.

The venue owns provisioning, binding, custody correctness and recovery. The
conductor need not discover or inspect how those systems work. Availability is
not merely a boolean: limitations, cost, capability, relevant personal continuity
and timing can change a casting decision. Consume that useful description, not
an installation or proof dossier.

If a requested musician or guarantee is unavailable, decide whether to use
another offered capability, reshape the performance, pause the affected work or
refer the limitation to the venue owner. Missing local files do not justify a
replacement package or custom lifecycle. An integrity stop remains a stop;
its technical resolution belongs to the maintainer under their own authority.

## Job control remains conducting

Submission, observation, pause, continuation and cancellation are ordinary
conducting controls. Use the supported venue controls within existing authority.
An emergency stop does not wait for paperwork. Where the venue cannot guarantee
that work has settled, hold conflicting replacement work and let the owner
resolve that limitation. Do not substitute process inspection for a missing
venue guarantee or infer child termination from a parent status alone.

Judge actual progress and outcome independently of a green job. Request the
missing substantive result or actionable status when the report is implausible.
The venue may diagnose sessions, bindings, validators and runtime state; ordinary
conducting does not inherit those procedures from `command` or another subskill.
No mandatory expert preflight is required to cast or run an offered engagement.

## Route faults by their effect

A useful exception identifies affected work, surviving results, current limits
and the choice needed. The venue owner diagnoses and restores service using its
existing systems. The conductor decides urgency, resource priority, acceptable
scope and whether independent work should continue. Missing enforcement is a
venue limitation, never an automatic conductor obligation. Do not impose a
manual counter, receipt join, snapshot audit or replacement checker to emulate it.

Retain successful child work when its wrapper fails. Ask the owner for the
supported continuation or recovered result; avoid rerunning successful work just
for a green wrapper. Accept only results backed by applicable evidence. A failed
wrapper does not erase a valid outcome, and a successful wrapper cannot create one.

## Commission new performance work

Use `composing` for a genuinely new or changed score/concert workflow and
`score-authoring` for its specialist review. Ask the composer to use the closest
existing examples and selected full Rosetta patterns, and explain the actual
behavioral gap requiring custom construction. Commission shared technical faults
to their existing owner; do not make a custom score a workaround for custody.

`marianne-expert` and technical `command` guidance belong to specialists when
technical discovery or maintenance is required. For an explicitly commissioned
venue role, read `references/venue-maintenance.md`. That route is optional for
ordinary conducting; importing a subskill never implicitly assigns both roles.
